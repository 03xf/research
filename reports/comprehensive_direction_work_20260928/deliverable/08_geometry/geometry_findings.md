# F/G/J — geometry and reliability

Three fire-linked Gold sites across two events are too few for a learned correction/rejection model. C0 B3_N is 13.283 m off; C1–C4 remain 11.535–12.676 m off despite favorable residual/condition/parallax. Visual correction improves only two of four historical fire-linked sites and worsens others. Free SfM is a comparison, not an absolute reference. The researchable signal is that internal residual/spread can fail to detect shared bias. F/G stay exploratory until independent calibration targets and held-site tests are available.
