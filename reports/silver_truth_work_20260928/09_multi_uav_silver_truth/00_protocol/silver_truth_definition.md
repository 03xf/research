# Silver-truth definition

Gold is an independently accepted physical LRF fire-source reference. B1_F1 and B3_N/S are three Gold sites across two fire events. B2_F1 is a prefire pile reference, not burning-fire Gold. B4 repeated LRF photos are one UAV observing one physical point and have no validated correspondence to the video two-source scene.

Silver is a source position triangulated from distinct UAVs with a declared input set, method, and uncertainty. Internal ray residual and subset stability are diagnostic, not proof of absolute accuracy. Only verified source instants enter the seed benchmark. Thirty-second overlapping video bins are sampling opportunities, not source observations. No Silver-A/B grade is assigned without Gold calibration and temporal validation.
