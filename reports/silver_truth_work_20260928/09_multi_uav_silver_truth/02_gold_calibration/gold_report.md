# Gold calibration

Three physical fire sites in two events are available: B1_F1, B3_N, B3_S. C0 full-view horizontal errors: 1.440, 13.283, 3.292 m (median 3.292, descriptive P90 11.285, P95 12.284, max 13.283). C0 two-support LOUO error: median 3.068, P90 13.454, P95 13.663, max 13.872 m across nine correlated subsets. Bias vectors by site are in gold_consensus_results.csv; pooled bias would hide site-specific error.

B2 is prefire pile only. B4 nine LRF observations are repeated observations by one UAV and cannot serve as multi-UAV Gold. Visual C1-C4 are available only for B3 and one instant. C3 equals C2 because the background-derived bound was inactive, so it supplies no independent success. Percentiles are descriptive for tiny clustered samples, not population uncertainty.
